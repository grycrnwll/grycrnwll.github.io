## Stationarity Trainer

The Stationarity Trainer is an interactive educational tool designed to help students and researchers understand the concept of stationarity in time series analysis. This tool provides:

- **Interactive Visualization**: Generate and visualize different types of time series data (stationary, non-stationary, trend-stationary)
- **Educational Content**: Learn about the importance of stationarity in econometric modeling
- **Hands-on Practice**: Test your understanding with various time series examples
- **Real-time Feedback**: Get immediate visual feedback on stationarity tests

### Features

- Generate synthetic time series with different stationarity properties
- Interactive plots showing original series, differenced series, and test statistics
